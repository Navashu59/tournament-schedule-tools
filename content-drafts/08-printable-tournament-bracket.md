# Printable Tournament Bracket

Use this page when the printed sheet is the thing people will follow at the event. The bracket should be readable on a clipboard, registration desk, wall, or classroom board without forcing players to decode a spreadsheet.

## Build for paper first

Enter the teams in the order you want them to appear, then generate the bracket before you print. For seeded events, keep the seed order manual. For a casual draw, shuffle once and leave the result alone so the printed copy matches what people saw.

## What a printed bracket must show

A useful printout needs more than matchup names. It should show the round, participant names, byes, venue or court, start time, and the path toward the final. If any of those details live in a separate message, people will ask the organizer for clarification.

## Make byes obvious

Byes are easy to miss on paper. Before printing, scan the first round and confirm which teams do not play immediately. If a top seed receives a bye, make sure that matches your published seeding rule.

## Export before printing when layout matters

Use CSV when you need to add logos, sponsor names, score boxes, or extra notes in a spreadsheet. Use Print directly when the browser table already contains everything your players need.

## Printable Tournament Bracket questions organizers ask

**What happens when the team count is not a power of two?**
The bracket needs byes to reach a clean bracket size. Review the printed first round so the bye teams are not mistaken for missing teams.

**Should I use manual seeding or shuffle?**
Use manual seeding when rankings, standings, or ratings matter. Use shuffle only for social events where a random draw is acceptable.

**What should be large enough to read?**
Round names, team names, court labels, and start times should be readable without zooming. If names are long, export CSV and adjust the layout before printing.

## How to read the generated output

The preview above shows the first matches and the path toward later rounds. After entering your teams, check that top seeds do not meet too early and that byes are visible before you print.

## Final review before you publish

Run this paper-specific review before you share the output:

- **Format:** The bracket should make seed order, byes, and the path to the final easy to understand.
- **Names:** Remove duplicates and fix spelling before generating the final copy.
- **Venue:** Check court count, start time, match length, breaks, and back-to-back matches.
- **Publishing:** Print once or export CSV and scan the table before sending it to players.
